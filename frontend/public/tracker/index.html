How these numbers are made

Seasonal forecast. ECMWF SEAS5 started 1 October 2026, 25 members, bias-corrected day by day against CHIRPS 1993–2016 by quantile mapping. Observed CHIRPS rainfall replaces the forecast up to the latest observed day in every year, so skill is measured the same way it is used.

Onset definitions. Dunning onset is the turning point of accumulated rainfall anomaly over 1 Sep – 31 Dec. Planting onset is the first day from 1 Oct with at least 20 mm in 1–3 days and no run of 10 days under 1 mm in the following 21 days.

Probabilities and skill. Early, normal and late are the thirds of the 1993–2016 CHIRPS record at each cell, calibrated toward one-third each where past forecasts were weak. Local skill compares past forecasts at the site's cell with CHIRPS: useful, limited or no skill.

Next 15 days. ECMWF IFS ensemble (open data, 50 members), not bias-corrected. The combined planting onset continues each member with a seasonal member to check for dry spells beyond day 15.

Satellite products. ARC2 (NOAA CPC), TAMSAT v3.1, PDIR-Now (UC Irvine CHRS) and CHIRPS (preliminary after September), averaged onto the same 0.25° grid. IMERG and GSMaP need accounts and are not included yet.

Seasonal conditioning. Where no product shows an onset, members with an onset before the ensemble start date are dropped and the probabilities recomputed.
